Three short tours

1 · Blood bank

The AI shortage outlook and donor matching
  1. The dashboard warns O− is already below the safe level, and says why.
  2. Click Mobilise O− donors → Find matching donors. Each donor gets a score with the reasons behind it.
  3. Send alerts → Simulate donor replies (demo) → Mark donated. Stock goes up.
  4. AI Models: forecast accuracy, the fairness check and the PDN rules.

2 · Donor

Find, check, book, and understand your blood test
  1. Find Campaigns → Use my location: map, distances, Book a slot.
  2. Eligibility Check: 15 questions; the rules decide and the AI explains the result.
  3. HemoScan AI → open a past report, or try a fictional sample PDF.
  4. Notifications → answer an urgent request with I can come.

3 · Drive organizer

Plan drives where the blood is needed
  1. Insights & Planner: where and when to hold the next drive, and regular donors to invite back.
  2. Create this campaign, then drop a pin on the map (or Find venue on map).
  3. Bookings & Check-in: mark donors as donated or no-show.
