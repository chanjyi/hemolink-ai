Privacy notice

Notis privasi · Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010

Version 2026-11-08 · HemoLink AI (student hackathon prototype)

Prototype. HemoLink AI is a student project for DSH Hacks V2. Please don't enter real health information on a public demo. Before real use, this notice must be reviewed by the operating blood bank and a PDPA adviser. Prototaip — jangan masukkan maklumat kesihatan sebenar pada demo awam.

1. What we collect · Data yang dikumpul

DataWhyWho can see it

2. Your choices · Pilihan anda

  • Blood type, birth year, weight, sex, phone and HemoScan are optional. Without them some features won't work.
  • Email, SMS, push and "new drives near me" alerts can each be switched off in My Profile → Alerts & language.
  • HemoScan asks for your agreement every time you upload a report. We keep a record of what you agreed to and when.

3. Who we share it with · Pendedahan

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Service providers only when you turn the feature on: the email provider, SMS provider (Twilio) and your browser's push service carry your messages; the AI provider (if the operator sets one) receives your first name, your question and the report values or donation status it needs to answer — never your full name, email, phone or location.
  • Map tiles and "Find venue on map" use OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

4. How we protect it · Keselamatan

  • Uploaded blood test files are encrypted on the server. Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2, 310,000 rounds).
  • Signed session cookies (HTTPS-only in production), a strict content security policy, rate limits and a 15-minute lock after 5 wrong passwords.
  • Role checks on every request: organizers and admins can't open anyone's HemoScan reports or medicine list.

5. How long we keep it · Penyimpanan

  • Original report files: deleted automatically after 12 months (the values you confirmed stay so you can see trends). Delete any report yourself at any time.
  • Message delivery log: 90 days.
  • Everything else: until you delete your account.

6. Your rights · Hak anda

  • See and copy your data — My Profile → Download my data (a JSON file).
  • Correct it — edit your profile, medicine list or report values at any time.
  • Delete it — My Profile → Delete my account removes your account, bookings, donation history, reports and medicines.
  • Withdraw consent — turn alerts off, delete reports, or delete your account.

7. Contact · Hubungi

Questions or requests: the blood bank running this HemoLink service. For this prototype, contact the HemoLink team at the email shown on the project page.
